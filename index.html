Academic Programs Overview

English Version and Bangla Medium, from Pre-Play through SSC, on one campus.

Early Years & Primary

Pre-Play and Early I–III follow an international early-years approach. Primary builds confident readers, careful thinkers, and kind classmates in both English Version and Bangla Medium.

Secondary

National curriculum through Class X, with spoken English, Singapore Math, science labs, ICT and character education — preparing students for Dhaka Board SSC.

Higher Secondary (O-Level / A-Level)

CMA currently educates through SSC. HSC affiliation is in process so students can continue under the same roof. University counselling already looks beyond the board exam.

Recognition

Merit Scholarships & Academic Excellence Awards

At Chetona Model Academy, academic excellence is part of ordinary school life — not a slogan for prize day. From Pre-Play through SSC, English Version and Bangla Medium classes share the same expectation: careful work, honest effort, and results that stand up in Dhaka Board examinations.

Scholarship and award programmes are written so families know the criteria in advance. They recognise board performance, subject mastery, and character. Notices are posted each session; the office will walk a parent through eligibility if the wording is unclear.

Distinguished scholarship & award opportunities

  • CMA Merit Scholarship — a session-wise fee concession for high achievement, reviewed each year with the class teacher’s report.
  • SSC Academic Excellence Awards — gold, silver, and class prizes after public results, including GPA-5 and subject excellence.
  • Talent Pool & Board Scholarship support — documentation and coaching for students who qualify for government talent-pool and general scholarships.
  • Subject & character awards — English, mathematics, science, and service honours presented at annual prize day.
