Campus & Facilities Highlights

Campus

The Mirpur campus

Chetona Model Academy stands on a single, well-kept campus in Mirpur — from Pre-Play through SSC, under one roof and one standard of care. English Version and Bangla Medium families arrive at the same gate, welcomed by the CMA banner and a community that knows its children by name.

Laboratories

Science on the bench

In the laboratory, curiosity is given proper tools: microscopes, careful measurement, and the discipline of testing an idea before writing it down. Students learn to observe, record, and explain — habits that serve them in public examinations and long after.

Study

Library & study garden

The library is staffed and quiet, with tables for independent work and story corners for younger readers. A shaded study garden offers a calm outdoor bay at break — so reading is not confined to the timetable, but becomes a natural part of the school day.

Daily life

Morning assembly

Each morning the campus gathers in the courtyard: a brief, orderly assembly for notices, song, and a shared beginning. It is a small ritual with a lasting purpose — children learn to stand together, listen well, and start the day as one school.

Culture

Arts on campus

Pohela Boishakh, Victory Day, and student performances fill the hall with music, recitation, and colour. Families are welcome; dates are published on the events calendar. Culture here is not an extra — it is how children learn belonging, language, and pride in their heritage.

Classrooms

Rooms built for teaching

Classrooms are bright, orderly, and designed for attention: calm desks, clear instruction, and teachers who stay with a task until it is finished. From Early Years through SSC, both streams share the same expectation of careful work and respectful conduct.

Sport

Playground & PE

Physical education belongs on the weekly timetable, not only on Sports Day. Football, cricket, badminton, and athletics run as seasonal programmes, so children learn stamina, fair play, and the satisfaction of practising as a team.

Clubs

Scouts & service

Scouts and service clubs give after-school hours a clear purpose: uniform, outdoor skill, and responsibility beyond the classroom. Students learn to lead quietly, help others, and carry CMA’s values into the wider community.
