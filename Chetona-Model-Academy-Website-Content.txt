Campus & Facilities Highlights
------------------------------
A single campus in Mirpur, designed for learning, play and gathering — smart classrooms, labs, library and a CCTV-monitored site.

    The Mirpur campus
    A secure, CCTV-monitored campus in Mirpur — from Pre-Play through SSC, under one roof and one standard of care. English Version and Bangla Medium families arrive at the same gate, welcomed by the CMA banner and a community that knows its children by name.

    Science and mathematics labs
    Modern mathematics and science laboratories give curiosity proper tools: measurement, experiment, and the discipline of testing an idea before writing it down. Students learn to observe, record and explain.

    Library & study garden
    The library is staffed and quiet, with tables for independent work and story corners for younger readers. A shaded study garden offers a calm outdoor bay at break — so reading is not confined to the timetable, but becomes a natural part of the school day.

    Morning assembly
    Each morning the campus gathers in the courtyard: a brief, orderly assembly for notices, song, and a shared beginning. It is a small ritual with a lasting purpose — children learn to stand together, listen well, and start the day as one school.

    Arts on campus
    Pohela Boishakh, Victory Day, and student performances fill the hall with music, recitation, and colour. Families are welcome; dates are published on the events calendar. Culture here is not an extra — it is how children learn belonging, language, and pride in their heritage.

    Smart classrooms
    Interactive smart classrooms keep attention on the lesson: clear instruction, modern teaching tools, and teachers who stay with a task until it is finished. A computer lab with a one-student, one-computer layout and an AI Learning Centre prepares students for digital work.

    Kids’ play zone
    A kids’ play zone and activity areas sit beside the playground. Football, cricket, badminton and athletics run as seasonal programmes, so children learn stamina, fair play and the satisfaction of practising as a team.

    Scouts & service
    Scouts and service clubs give after-school hours a clear purpose: uniform, outdoor skill, and responsibility beyond the classroom. Students learn to lead quietly, help others, and carry CMA’s values into the wider community.

History
------------

    Founding Story
    Chetona Model Academy started its journey in 2005 with the pride of the first English Version academic institution in the Mirpur area, with the purpose of spreading quality global education. The campus remains at Plot # 2, Road # 4, Block-B, Sec-2 — the plot families still walk to each morning.

    Milestones & Timeline
    • 2005 CMA opens in Mirpur as an English Version school.
    • Pre-school to Class X International early-years curriculum (Early I–III) alongside national curriculum from Class 1 to SSC. Bangla Medium runs in parallel.
    • Dhaka Board Affiliation through SSC (EIIN 132109). HSC affiliation is in process.
    • Public examinations Strong SSC performance, including a 100% public exam pass rate and 48% SSC GPA-5 in 2025.
    • Today More than 1,200 students and 90+ teachers on one campus — Knowledge, Wisdom, Character.

Student life where every day is inspiring.
------------------------------------------
Wide-ranging extra-curricular activity — competitions, culture, science fairs, sport and study tours — so children grow into well-rounded individuals.

Facilities
------------
A secure, CCTV-monitored campus for Bangla Medium and English Version — smart classrooms, labs, library, kids’ play zone and care on one site.

    Laboratories
    Modern mathematics and science laboratories, plus a computer lab with a one-student, one-computer layout and an AI Learning Centre.

    Library
    A staffed library with quiet tables, story corners for younger children, and reference shelves for examination classes.

    Sports Complex
    Playground, kids’ play zone, indoor games space and activity areas for house matches and the annual sports meet.

    Transport
    Supervised school vans with published routes. Drivers and attendants are known to the office by name.

    Cafeteria
    A simple, hygienic canteen with a short daily menu. Packed lunches from home are always welcome.

    Medical & Health Room
    A first-aid room with a trained attendant during school hours. Parents are called if a child needs to go home.

Clubs & Activities
------------------

    Clubs that discover talent
    CMA hosts vibrant clubs so children learn collaboration and build character.

    Science Club
    For young innovators, experimenters and tech enthusiasts — experiments, science week and fairs.

    Debate Club
    Critical thinking, logic and public speaking, from prepared motions to impromptu rounds.

    Chess & Indoor Games
    Focus, strategy and healthy sportsmanship through chess and indoor games.

    Arts Club
    Drawing, painting, and exhibition work for campus displays and national days.

    Music Club
    Choir, instrumental practice, and performance at assembly and annual functions.

    Cultural Club
    Language, heritage, and festival programmes — plus community service beyond the classroom.

A school that feels like home
-----------------------------
Children learn best when they feel happy, safe and respected. Our teachers build caring relationships, creating classrooms that are collaborative and competitive — enthusiasm, teamwork and leadership, with achievements celebrated and every child valued.
This warm, supportive environment makes CMA more than a school. It becomes a second home.

Sports & Co-Curricular
----------------------
Games and PE belong to every class — Bangla Medium and English Version share the same field, houses, and Sports Day.

    Sports Programs
    Physical education is on the weekly timetable. Football, cricket, badminton, and athletics run as seasonal programmes, with house teams from Class 3 upwards.

    • House teams

    Annual Sports Events
    Sports Day is a full-campus morning of track events, relays, and family attendance. Inter-house trophies are awarded the same afternoon in the auditorium.
    • Track events
    • Relays
    • House trophies
    • Families welcome

Code of Conduct
---------------
The same expectations for Bangla Medium and English Version — punctual, in uniform, courteous, and honest in work.

    Student Code of Conduct
    Students are expected to be on time, in uniform, polite in speech, and honest in work. Mobile phones are not used during the school day without permission. The full code is given at enrolment.
    • Punctual
    • Uniform
    • Courtesy
    • Honest work
    • No phones without permission

    Disciplinary Policy
    We prefer restoration over spectacle. Concerns go first to the class teacher, then to section heads. Serious matters are written down and shared with parents the same day.
    • Class teacher first
    • Then section heads
    • Written record
    • Parents informed same day

Student Leadership

Leadership is service. Bangla Medium and English Version students share the same council, houses, and duties.

    Student Council

    • Prefects

    Leadership Roles & Responsibilities
    House captains, library monitors, and club secretaries have written role descriptions. Leadership here means showing up, listening, and doing the quiet work well.
    • House captains

    • Club secretaries

Alumni Network

    The Chetona Alumni Association keeps former students connected to one another and to the campus — whether you studied in Bangla Medium or English Version. We host an annual gathering, share university and career news, and invite alumni to speak in assemblies when they can.

    • News, not rumours University and career notes go to registered members, not through informal chains.

    Membership is open to anyone who completed a stage of study here. Register so we can write to you with dates.
