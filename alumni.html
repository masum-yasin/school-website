About the Alumni Association

The Chetona Alumni Association keeps former students connected to one another and to the campus. We host an annual gathering, share university and career news, and invite alumni to speak in assemblies when they can.

Membership is open to anyone who completed a stage of study here. Register below so we can write to you with dates, not rumours.
