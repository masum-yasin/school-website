Life at CMA

Climb Toward the Light of Ideas

Every CMA journey starts with curiosity. Step by step — through learning, clubs, challenges and friendship — students climb toward brighter ideas, stronger character and a wider future.

  • Curiosity that lights up every school day
  • Steady steps from Pre-Play to SSC success
  • Support that helps every learner rise higher

A Day of Purposeful Learning

Classroom hours stay energetic and clear — building skills that matter for exams and for life.

  • Spoken English, Phonics & confident presentation
  • Singapore Math with modern Math Lab practice
  • Science experiments that spark curiosity
  • Digital literacy & age-appropriate AI awareness
  • Character lessons woven into everyday manners

Active Play, Energy & Friendship

After focused classroom hours, CMA children move, play and grow together — building balance, confidence and joyful bonds beyond books.

  • Outdoor games, sports & healthy physical activity
  • Balance, coordination and team spirit through play
  • Friendship that grows with every shared adventure
  • Arts, music & stage moments that free expression

Starting the Day with Unity

Every CMA morning begins together. In the courtyard assembly, students stand side by side — from Pre-Play to Class X — sharing the national anthem, prayer and a clear purpose for the day ahead.

  • Discipline, respect and a shared school spirit
  • Patriotism and values practiced every morning
  • Announcements that keep the whole campus connected
  • A sense of belonging that lasts beyond the classroom

Proud Moments & Bright Faces

From medals and certificates to quiet classroom focus — CMA celebrates effort at every age. These are real campus moments of joy, grit and growing confidence.

  • Trophies, medals & certificates earned with teamwork
  • Young learners smiling through books and practice
  • Focus that builds foundations for excellence
  • Recognition that motivates the next challenge

Class Parties & Celebration

Learning feels lighter when joy is shared. Class parties bring colourful dress, balloons and teacher–student bonding — memories that make school a second home.

  • Festive class events that build belonging
  • Teachers celebrating milestones with every section
  • Culture, friendship and happy campus moments
  • Confidence grown through shared celebrations

Guided Learning & Care

At CMA, teachers lean in — literally. With a close 1:13 teacher–student ratio, every child gets patient guidance, clear feedback and the confidence to try again.

  • One-to-one support that maps each learner’s pace
  • Collaborative desks where classmates grow together
  • Warm mentoring that builds trust, not pressure
  • Parent partnership for steady, confident progress
