Academics

Teaching that is ambitious and humane.

From the first years through Higher Secondary, the curriculum is designed so that children build knowledge in sequence — and never lose the joy of finding something out.

Framework

Curriculum Overview

A written scheme of work from Pre-Play to SSC, so knowledge is built in sequence and skills travel from one year to the next.

Curriculum Framework

Each stage has a written scheme of work. Language and mathematics are daily anchors; science and social studies are ways of seeing the world; the arts and physical education are essential, not optional. Teachers plan together so that skills travel from one year to the next.

National curriculum runs from Class 1 to SSC in English Version and Bangla Medium. Pre-school (Early I–III) follows an international early-years curriculum. HSC affiliation is in process so families can continue on the same campus.

Language & Mathematics

Daily anchors for literacy and number sense in both English Version and Bangla Medium.

Science & Social Studies

Practical science and a wider view of people, place, and history — ways of seeing the world.

Arts & Physical Education

Art, music, and sport sit on the weekly timetable — essential parts of the school day, not extras.

Year-on-year planning

Teachers plan together so that skills are practised, deepened, and carried forward from one year to the next.

Assessment

Examinations

Exam Routine

Term assessments and annual examinations are published on the academic calendar and issued as a dated routine to each class. Students receive the timetable at least two weeks in advance.

Marking Criteria

Scripts are marked against published criteria. Teachers comment on what is secure and what should be practised next. Grade descriptors are shared with parents at the first meeting of the year.

Result Publication

Results are released through the parent portal and as printed reports. Queries are received in writing within seven working days of publication.

Stages

Programs

Early Years / Primary

Small-step phonics, number sense, and plenty of talk. Children learn to work with others, to finish a task, and to take pride in neat work.

Secondary

Specialist teaching, practical science, and a wider reading culture. Students begin to choose how they study as well as what they study.

Higher Secondary (O-Level / A-Level)

CMA currently prepares students through SSC under Dhaka Education Board. HSC affiliation is in process so families can continue on the same campus. Counselling already looks toward university.
