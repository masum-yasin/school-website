Academics

Academic excellence. Learning that inspires.

English Version and Bangla Medium, from Pre-Play through SSC. A well-researched curriculum, practical learning and caring teachers prepare students for the next examination — and for life beyond it.

Framework

Curriculum Overview

A written scheme of work from Pre-Play to SSC, so knowledge is built in sequence and skills travel from one year to the next.

Curriculum Framework

At CMA, education is more than excellent examination results. Students develop knowledge, skills and confidence through a well-researched curriculum — engaging classroom teaching, regular assessment and practical learning, so they are ready for the next examination and for future challenges.

National curriculum runs from Class 1 to SSC in English Version and Bangla Medium. Pre-school (Early I–III) follows an international early-years curriculum. Science and Commerce groups are offered at secondary level. HSC affiliation is in process so families can continue on the same campus.

Language & Mathematics

Spoken English and structured phonics from the early years; Singapore Mathematics and a modern Math Lab so children understand and enjoy number work.

Science & Social Studies

Science through discovery: observe, experiment and explore. Practical lab activities connect classroom learning with the real world.

Arts & Physical Education

Art, music, and sport sit on the weekly timetable — essential parts of the school day, not extras.

Digital & future-ready

Early digital literacy, age-appropriate coding logic and foundational AI awareness in a one-student, one-computer lab.

The year

Academic Calendar

Term dates, exams, and holidays for Bangla Medium and English Version — the same year for every class.

Term Dates

Term 1

10 August 2026 to 10 December 2026

First term of the session, including mid-year assessments in October.

Term 2

4 January 2027 to 15 April 2027

Second term, with mid-term assessments in February.

Term 3

26 April 2027 to 24 June 2027

Final term, including annual examinations for most classes in May.

Examination Schedule

Mid-term tests are in October and February. Annual exams for most classes are in May. Public exam dates follow the Dhaka Education Board timetable for both streams.

Holiday List

National holidays, Eid recesses, and a winter break are published each July. An updated copy is available from the school office and on the notice board.
