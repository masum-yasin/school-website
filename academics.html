The CMA difference

Knowledge, skills and confidence

Education at Chetona Model Academy is more than examination results. Engaging classroom teaching, regular assessment and practical learning — with moments of play — prepare students for the next test and for future challenges.

Following the national curriculum from Class I to SSC, with international-style early years (Early-I to Early-III), our teachers remain devoted to global-quality learning under disciplined leadership.

  • Structured Spoken English & phonics from early years
  • Concept-based Mathematics with Modern Math Lab
  • Hands-on science discovery and digital literacy
  • Character education woven into daily school life
