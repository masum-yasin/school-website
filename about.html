Our story

A Few Words About CMA

Chetona Model Academy (CMA) began in 2005 as one of the first English-version academic institutions in Mirpur — built to spread quality, values-based education among young learners.

Today CMA offers a complete Pre-Play to SSC pathway in English Version and Bangla Medium, with strong results in public examinations and a caring culture of Knowledge, Wisdom and Character.

Under disciplined leadership and about 90 trained teachers serving 1200+ students, we prepare Chetonians for higher education and for life — as confident speakers, thoughtful citizens and lifelong learners.

Who we are

A Symbol of Discipline & Excellence

Chetona Model Academy began in 2005 as one of Mirpur’s pioneering English-version schools — growing into a trusted Pre-Play to SSC pathway in English Version and Bangla Medium, with Science and Commerce groups.

Today about 90 trained teachers guide 1200+ students in a safe, CCTV-monitored campus under caring leadership. We turn classroom teaching into practice so children build knowledge, character, creativity and communication.
