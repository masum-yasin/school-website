About Us

A school with a clear purpose.

Chetona Model Academy exists so that children in Mirpur can grow into capable, considerate young people — with parents as partners at every step.

Leadership

Chairman's Message

Welcome Note

On behalf of the Governing Body, I welcome you to Chetona Model Academy. Since 2005 CMA has stood in Mirpur as a school families can trust — academically serious, and human in its care.

Chairman's Vision for the Institution

Our vision is that CMA students will go on to general universities, engineering universities, medical colleges and other higher institutions at home and abroad — carrying knowledge, wisdom and character from this campus.

Leadership

Principal's Message

Welcome Note

Dear parents, welcome to Chetona Model Academy — a school where every child is valued, encouraged and inspired to succeed. Walk our corridors at mid-morning and you will hear discussion, not noise for its own sake.

Principal's Educational Philosophy

We go beyond the traditional curriculum. Classroom teaching becomes practice so students develop knowledge, character, creativity and communication. Our teachers partner with parents to help every child become a responsible citizen and a lifelong learner. — Brig Gen Md Mehdi Hassan (Retd), Principal

Purpose

Mission, Vision & Values

Our Mission

To provide a complete K–12 education that is academically strong, morally grounded, and open to every family who shares our commitment to hard work and respect.

Our Vision

To be the school families in Dhaka recommend without hesitation — known for thoughtful teaching, a calm campus, and graduates who contribute.

Core Values

Integrity, curiosity, compassion, discipline, and partnership with parents. These five guide assemblies, classrooms, and the way we speak to children.

Our story

History

Founding Story

Chetona Model Academy started its journey in 2005 with the pride of the first English Version academic institution in the Mirpur area, with the purpose of spreading quality global education. The campus remains at Plot # 2, Road # 4, Block-B, Sec-2 — the plot families still walk to each morning.

Milestones & Timeline

  1. 2005 CMA opens in Mirpur as an English Version school.
  2. Pre-school to Class X International early-years curriculum (Early I–III) alongside national curriculum from Class 1 to SSC. Bangla Medium runs in parallel.
  3. Dhaka Board Affiliation through SSC (EIIN 132109). HSC affiliation is in process.
  4. Public examinations Strong SSC performance, including a 99.8% public exam pass rate and 48% SSC GPA-5 in 2025.
  5. Today More than 1,200 students and 90+ teachers on one campus — Knowledge, Wisdom, Character.

Place

Campus & Facilities

A compact, walkable campus in Mirpur — classrooms around shared courtyards, shade for play, and rooms set aside for science, reading, and performance.

Laboratories

Science and ICT labs for practical work from upper primary onwards, with safety taught before the first experiment.

Library

A quiet reading room with age-banded collections in Bangla and English, and tables for study at break.

Playground

Open ground for games, sports day, and daily recreation — PE sits on the weekly timetable.

Auditorium

A gathering hall for assemblies, concerts, prize day, and parent meetings.

Transport

Supervised routes across Mirpur and neighbouring areas, with a published timetable from the office.

Cafeteria

A simple, hygienic canteen with a short daily menu. Packed lunches from home are always welcome.

Recognition

Accreditations & Affiliations

Board affiliation, public results, and a dual-medium curriculum — the measures we hold ourselves to.

Recognitions

CMA is an English Version and Bangla Medium school. Public examination results, inspection visits, and the standing of our families remain the measures we hold ourselves to — not a list of slogans.

Curriculum body / Education Board

Dhaka Education Board affiliation is in place through SSC. National curriculum runs from Class 1 to SSC; pre-school (Early I–III) follows an international early-years curriculum. HSC affiliation is in process so students can continue under the same roof.
