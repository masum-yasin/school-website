How to join

Admission Process

Eligibility Criteria

Places are offered by age and previous schooling. For Early Years, children should meet the age cut-off published in the current circular. For later years, a recent report card and a placement conversation help us recommend the right class.

Step-by-Step Process

  1. Enquire or visit. Call the office or submit the enquiry form on the Contact page.
  2. Collect the circular. Fee notes, seat availability, and test dates are issued each session.
  3. Submit the application. Use the online form below or a paper form from the office.
  4. Assessment & meeting. Age-appropriate tasks and a conversation with parents.
  5. Offer & enrolment. Successful families receive a written offer and a deadline to confirm.

Required Documents

  • Birth certificate or valid age proof
  • Two passport-size photographs of the student
  • Previous school report (if transferring)
  • Parents’ national ID copies
  • Immunisation record for Early Years applicants
