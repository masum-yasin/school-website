How to join

Admission Process

Your child’s bright future begins here. Visit the campus, meet our teachers and experience the caring environment families have trusted for nearly two decades.

Eligibility Criteria

Places are offered by age and previous schooling. For Early Years, children should meet the age cut-off in the current circular. For later years, a recent report card and a placement conversation help us recommend the right class.

Step-by-Step Process

  1. 1

    Visit the website and the school

    Read about CMA online, then book a campus visit so your family can see the school at work.

  2. 2

    Meet our Admission Team

    The office will be pleased to guide you. Call the Admission Office on 01953-767002.

  3. 3

    Complete the formalities

    Fill in the admission form, sit the admission test where required, and collect books after a place is offered.

  4. 4

    Enrolment

    Our Admission Office stays with you through every remaining step until your child is on the roll.

Required Documents

  • Birth certificate or valid age proof
  • Two passport-size photographs of the student
  • Previous school report (if transferring)
  • Parents’ national ID copies
  • Immunisation record for Early Years applicants

Scholarships & Financial Assistance

A limited number of merit scholarships and need-based bursaries are awarded each year. Families may write to the Chairman’s office with supporting documents. Awards are confidential and reviewed annually.
